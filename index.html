What I do

Five disciplines that separate AI that demos from AI that ships.

01 · AI PRODUCT MANAGEMENT & ENTERPRISE TRANSFORMATION

From boardroom question to production P&L, and adoption that sticks

When executives ask where to apply AI, I return a prioritized portfolio, a quantified business case and value proposition for each, and the delivery plan through compliance into production, including the honest call on which problems do not need AI at all. Then the enterprise transformation work: assessing end to end workflows across claims, underwriting and distribution, redesigning processes around AI, and running the change management and adoption tracking that turns launches into sustained usage.

Built in my work: fraud networks, claims automation and advisor AI running in production at RBC Insurance. Additionally: four open-sourced strategy engagements showing the method
02 · LLM GATEWAY & COST ENGINEERING

One gateway, any model, a defensible unit cost

Provider-agnostic model routing, token budgets per interaction, context compaction, payload offloading, and run-cost forecasting the CFO can plan on. I treat the context window and the invoice as engineered resources, not surprises.

Built in my work: token cost optimization and run-cost forecasting for production AI at a major bank. Additionally open-sourced: a working gateway across Anthropic, OpenAI, Bedrock, Vertex and Ollama
03 · AIDLC · THE OPERATING DISCIPLINE

An SDLC built for AI, open-sourced

The Applied AI Delivery Lifecycle: structured discovery, quantified business cases, five executive decision gates, evaluation harnesses, and governance mapped to EU AI Act risk tiers and NIST AI RMF. Rolled out as enterprise enablement tooling with a community of practice across squads, it is how an organization ships AI the same way twice.

Built in my work: the gated delivery discipline I run with squads day to day. Additionally open-sourced: applied-ai-enterprise · PyPI: aidlc-studio
04 · AI EVALS & GOVERNANCE

Evals are the license to operate

I design the evaluation gates that get risk and compliance to yes: pass/fail bars agreed before build, prompt-injection resistance, bias checks, drift and override telemetry in production, and the audit trail that survives a regulator. In a regulated enterprise, the eval framework is the product decision.

Built in my work: the framework that moved fraud, claims and advisor AI past model risk review at a major bank. Additionally open-sourced as eval harnesses in AIDLC
05 · DATA PIPELINES & DATA PRODUCTS

AI-ready data products from any cloud, any lake

Developed an integrated data pipeline connecting multi-cloud sources and multiple data lakes, Snowflake and Databricks, on a Medallion architecture: ingesting and processing both structured and unstructured data (documents, images, transcripts, transactions) into governed, AI-ready data products. This is the layer that makes every applied AI use case above possible, because models are only as trustworthy as the data products beneath them.

Built in my work: the data foundation feeding fraud GNNs, claims document intelligence and advisor retrieval across 15+ insurance domains and 80+ systems of record
