Six agents mirror a top-tier consulting engagement: diagnostic, value pools, use-case portfolio, business cases, operating model, executive readout. About

Before that: conversational AI and content product strategy at Bell, computer vision loss prevention at Walmart Canada, and eight years of client-facing product delivery at IBM. The through line is regulated, customer-facing scale.
